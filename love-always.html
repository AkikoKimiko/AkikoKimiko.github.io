AKIKO KIMIKO · 2025 · ART 001

LOVE: ALWAYS.

Love is what makes us who we are — and what connects us.

The story behind the art

Countless worlds. Countless little universes, scattered across the endless vastness of space. Each one existing on its own, yet connected in ways the eye can barely comprehend. From the darkness, a blossom rises. Its stem winds like a strand of DNA, like the invisible thread connecting all forms of life. A tiny, rocky island drifting through a sea of stars and possibilities. Because sometimes, the most beautiful things do not exist on the surface. They grow deep within us, hidden in our very essence — until they find their way into the light. Perhaps that is what love is: not something we must find or learn, but something that has always lived within us. It is part of what makes us who we are — and at the same time, the invisible thread that connects us with all life. A quiet glow travelling through countless worlds. Sometimes visible, sometimes hidden. Yet never truly gone. Some worlds may drift apart. But love remains — in all that we are, and all that connects us. Love. Always.

The lettering and selected particles are painted with fluorescent colour, revealing another kind of glow in the dark.
