AKIKO KIMIKO · 2026 · ART 001

EASE

A State of Being

The story behind the art

Sometimes, the simplest moments are the ones that feel the most beautiful. A quiet evening. A little time to oneself. Two people, each in their own little world, yet connected all the same. Nothing to do. Nothing to chase. Nothing to wait for. Just a quiet sense of belonging to the moment. The freedom to enjoy whatever brings you happiness, knowing that someone is there. Perhaps balance is exactly that: not having to keep everything in perfect harmony, but simply feeling at peace within yourself. A state without effort. Without expectation. Simply being. EASE – A STATE OF BEING.
