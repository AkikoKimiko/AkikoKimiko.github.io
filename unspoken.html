AKIKO KIMIKO · 2025 · ART 001

UNSPOKEN

Where no words are needed, because everything has been said.

The story behind the art

Two souls finding each other. The moment they recognize one another. As if they had shared a thousand lifetimes and found their way back to each other again and again. When two worlds meet and something emerges that is greater than the sum of its parts. A magic that cannot be explained and needs no words. Sometimes, we do not meet someone for the first time. We simply remember them. Because sometimes, everything has already been said without a single word.
